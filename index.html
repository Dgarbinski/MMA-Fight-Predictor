HOW IT WORKS

A repeatable model for every UFC card.

MMA Fight Predictor uses historical fighter and bout information to compare upcoming matchups. The result is presented as a predicted winner, confidence percentage, and method-of-victory split.

Predictions are updated as event information changes, giving you a consistent way to review a card without sorting through raw fight statistics yourself.

  1. 1
    Collect matchup data

    Upcoming UFC bouts and available fighter history are organized for analysis.

  2. 2
    Run the prediction model

    The matchup is evaluated using the same model framework across the card.

  3. 3
    Display the probabilities

    Winner confidence and method-of-victory projections are shown in the app.

MMA & UFC PREDICTIONS

Looking for a UFC fight predictor app?

MMA Fight Predictor is designed for people searching for UFC fight predictions, MMA matchup analysis, method-of-victory projections, confidence-based picks, and an easy way to compare fights on an upcoming card.

Instead of presenting a single unsupported pick, the app shows the model’s confidence and projected finish distribution, helping you understand how strongly the model favors each result.

FAQ

Common questions

What is MMA Fight Predictor?

It is an iPhone app that provides model-based predictions for UFC fights, including winner confidence and method-of-victory projections.

Does the app predict KO, submission, and decision outcomes?

Yes. Matchups can include projected probabilities for KO/TKO, submission, and decision outcomes.

Are UFC fight predictions guaranteed?

No. Combat sports are unpredictable. Model outputs are estimates, not guarantees of any fight result.

Where can I download MMA Fight Predictor?

The app is available on Apple’s App Store for compatible iPhone devices.
