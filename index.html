HOW IT WORKS

A repeatable model for every supported UFC card.

MMA Fight Predictor uses historical fighter and bout information to compare upcoming matchups. The result is presented as a predicted winner, confidence percentage, and method-of-victory split.

Predictions are updated as event information changes, giving you a consistent way to review a card without sorting through raw fight statistics yourself.

FAQ

Common questions about MMA Fight Predictor

What is MMA Fight Predictor?

It is an iPhone app that provides model-based predictions for supported UFC fights, including winner confidence and method-of-victory projections.

Does the app predict KO, submission, and decision outcomes?

Yes. Matchups can include projected probabilities for KO/TKO, submission, and decision outcomes.

Can I use the predictions with sportsbook odds?

You can compare model probability with sportsbook pricing as one part of your own analysis. A model pick is not the same thing as betting value, and predictions are never guarantees.

Does MMA Fight Predictor cover DWCS fights?

The app focuses on supported UFC events. This website includes DWCS prospect analysis because Contender Series fighters often move into future UFC matchups, but it does not claim that every DWCS bout is available as an in-app prediction.

Are UFC fight predictions guaranteed?

No. Combat sports are unpredictable. Model outputs are estimates, not guarantees of any fight result.

Where can I download MMA Fight Predictor?

MMA Fight Predictor is available on Apple's App Store for compatible iPhone devices.
