How to read these UFC picks

Each free pick shows the model's projected winner and confidence score. The KO/TKO, submission, and decision percentages describe the model's projected ways that selected fighter could win. They are probabilities, not guarantees.

Why are some picks locked?

A matchup becomes a Premium selection only when an eligible sportsbook offers a displayed line on the predicted winner that is better than the model's calculated break-even moneyline. Premium status can therefore change as sportsbook prices move.
